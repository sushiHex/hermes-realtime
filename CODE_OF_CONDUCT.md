# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, precise, and constructive. Disagreement about architecture, evidence, security, or scope is welcome; harassment and personal attacks are not.

Examples of acceptable behavior include:

- critiquing code and claims rather than people;
- stating uncertainty and limitations honestly;
- giving reproducible technical feedback;
- respecting privacy and disclosure boundaries;
- helping newcomers understand project constraints.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or unwanted sexual attention;
- publishing another person's private information;
- deliberately introducing secrets, personal data, or unsafe instructions;
- sustained disruption, bad-faith review, or retaliation;
- conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other material that violates this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly. Platform abuse can also be reported through GitHub's abuse-reporting tools.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, impact, prior behavior, and the safety of affected participants. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself a violation.
